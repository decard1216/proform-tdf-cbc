Running on iPhone & iPad

Apple's Safari blocks Web Bluetooth (navigator.bluetooth) on iOS and iPadOS. To connect wirelessly to your ProForm TDF CBC:

  1. Install the free Bluefy – Web BLE Browser app from the iOS App Store.
  2. Save this HTML file to iCloud Drive, GitHub Pages, or serve it from your PC on your local network.
  3. Wake up your TDF CBC by pedaling a few strokes, then open this page in Bluefy and tap Connect BLE.

Later, if you want a dedicated Home Screen app without Bluefy, you can wrap this exact HTML/JS in Capacitor with @capacitor-community/bluetooth-le.
